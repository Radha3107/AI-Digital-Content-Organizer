Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the AI Digital Content Organizer handles information when you use the application.

1. About the Application

AI Digital Content Organizer is a student project developed for educational purposes. The application uses machine learning to classify and organize digital content into categories such as Education, Technology, Fitness, Food, Travel, Gaming, Fashion, and Entertainment.

2. Information We Process

The application may process content information such as video titles, descriptions, URLs, categories, and other publicly available metadata obtained through supported platform APIs.

The application does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment information, or government identification documents.

3. YouTube Content

The application may use the YouTube Data API to retrieve publicly available video information such as video titles, channel names, thumbnails, and URLs.

YouTube content remains the property of its respective creators and is accessed through YouTube's services.

4. Pinterest Content

If Pinterest API functionality is enabled, the application may access Pinterest content and metadata permitted by the user's authorization and the permissions granted to the application.

Pinterest content remains the property of its respective creators and is accessed through Pinterest's services.

5. How Information Is Used

Information retrieved by the application is used for the following purposes:

  • Classifying digital content using a machine-learning model.
  • Organizing content into meaningful categories.
  • Displaying content and its source URL.
  • Allowing users to search and filter their organized content.
  • Supporting the educational demonstration of the project.

6. Local Storage

Content saved to the application's personal library may be stored locally by the application. This information is used to maintain the user's organized content library.

7. Third-Party Services

The application may interact with third-party services such as YouTube and Pinterest APIs. Their respective privacy policies and terms of service apply to information handled by those services.

For more information, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Pinterest Privacy Policy

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to prevent unauthorized access to information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

This educational project is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13, and it does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of the application changes. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

11. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the AI Digital Content Organizer project, please contact the project developer through the project's GitHub repository.
